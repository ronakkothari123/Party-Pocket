# 🎉 Party Pocket

**Party Pocket** is a multiplayer party game mobile app built with Flutter.  
It combines classic social and party games into one seamless, fast-setup experience designed for game nights, college hangouts, family gatherings, and road trips.

No accounts. No setup. Just pass the phone and play.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🎮 5 Built-In Party Games
- 👥 Supports 2–20+ players
- 🔊 Dynamic music & sound effects
- 📱 Offline-first gameplay (ads require internet)
- ⚡ Fast setup, no login required
- 📦 Built with Flutter
- 📢 Google AdMob monetization
- 🔐 Production-signed Android release

---

## 🎲 Game Modes

### 🔥 Hot Potato
Pass the phone before time runs out.  
Randomized background music, win-state sounds, and player seating visualization.

### 🌊 Wavelength
Ask each player a question, then guess where their answer falls on the spectrum.  
Optional category generator available on demand.

### 🦎 Chameleon
One player doesn’t know the secret word.  
Blend in, interrogate, and vote.

### ❓ 10 Questions
Deductive guessing game with a 10-question limit.

### 🎭 Heads Up–Style Mode
Hold the phone to your forehead and guess based on your friends’ clues.

## 🔐 Privacy

Party Pocket:

- Does **not** collect personal data
- Does **not** require accounts
- Does **not** store user information
- Runs offline (ads require internet)

Google AdMob is used for monetization and may collect Advertising ID for ad delivery and fraud prevention.
